Inbox Precheck – Privacy policy

Inbox Precheck is a personal tool used only by Dylan Larsen with his own Google accounts.

  • It requests read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete, or change any email.
  • It reads only the information needed to count new messages and note their message IDs. It does not store email subjects, bodies, senders, or attachments.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer and are never shared or sold.
  • No data is shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page.

Last updated: 24 November 2026.
